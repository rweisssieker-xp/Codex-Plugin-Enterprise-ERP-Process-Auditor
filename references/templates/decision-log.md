Record material and high-impact decisions only after a named human **Approver** has reviewed them. Link the decision to its supporting and conflicting Evidence IDs, identify a named **Validation Owner** for any high-impact recommendation, and describe a testable **Reversal Condition**.

This register supports traceability and review; it does not provide legal, tax, audit, security, or compliance assurance.
