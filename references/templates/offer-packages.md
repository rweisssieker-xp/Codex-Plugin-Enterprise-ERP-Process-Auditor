# Offer-package recommendation

Select one package only when the supplied evidence satisfies its rule. Record the evidence gaps or decision conditions that prevent a selection.

| Package | Select when | Deliverables and entry criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Rapid Diagnostic | limited evidence, one process, and a decision required within 0–90 days | Scoped diagnostic, candidate findings, and targeted evidence requests; requires one agreed process scope and a named decision question. |
| Process Deep Dive | sufficient evidence for root cause, controls, and quantified opportunities | Evidence-backed root-cause and control review with quantified opportunity scenarios; requires supplied process evidence and review access. |
| Transformation Portfolio | multiple processes or entities with initiative and roadmap decisions | Prioritized initiative portfolio, dependency roadmap, and decision pack; requires validated findings and accountable decision owners. |
| Continuous Process Intelligence | recurring evidence refresh and approved recurring KPI review | Human-reviewed recurring evidence and KPI review; requires an approved review cadence and named accountable reviewers. Make no unattended monitoring claim. |
